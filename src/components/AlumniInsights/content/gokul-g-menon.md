## Quick Intro

Air India came for placements in early October. This was after the first wave of companies that came during June-August. Air India involved 3 rounds after the initial coding test, first two being technical followed by a managerial HR.

Since my intention was to get placed at companies that deal with Machine Learning or data in general in some way rather than a pure software developer role (or a company that pays very well for the latter), Air India was only my 4rth company. Hence the following experience is mostly centered on getting through the Air India test.

Air India offered the following roles:

* Junior software developer
* ChatBot Developer
* Data Analyst
* Junior Quality Analyst
* Data Scientist
* Data Engineer

## Coding Round

This round was online and was how you’d expect a coding round to go. It covered many topics in CSE from DBMS, OS, DSA and Algorithm Analysis.

We had 3 coding questions out of which two were pretty easy and one was at a higher level being a dynamic programming problem. But mostly you can get away with answering just two of the three questions. This requires some leetcode practice in the medium section.

There was also a SQL problem which was fairly complex. Good practice with sql (including joins) will help you out a lot.

## First Interview round

In this round I had two interviewers. The round was for the most part, centered around my Resume. Since my resume only had projects which I had thorough in-depth knowledge about, I could get through most questions.

They were searching for various technologies I used as mentioned in my resume and asking questions based on that to see if all of that was for real. I remember two occasions of that.

When I mentioned SQL, they asked me to rate my knowledge in it. I went with an 8 and then they asked me a question on something I didn’t know. I circumvented the question with an alternate solution than what they expected with my knowledge level rating and my speaking skills really helped me out.

I had one mention of Google Cloud in my Resume which was the only thing I had barely any idea about. They asked me right away as they saw it but I was prepared for an “I don’t know” answer (framed better obviously) and they were fine.

Ultimately this round was about what I had written down as my resume and my ability to just handle the interview well.

## Second interview round

This round was also technical and a one on one interview. It was much more detailed than the last one.

I was one of the first to qualify for this round and my interviewer utilized all that extra time. The round lasted some 45 minutes or something (Don't remember exactly) and he went through each project in my entire resume down to its finest and most basic detail.

This might prolly be because my resume has a lot of stuff and gave him a lot of stuff to cover through hence dragging on for such length.

In this round however, they didn't make me write anything down as code which was not the case for everyone else in their second round (one friend was asked to write down react code for various features because his resume had react in it).

By the end though, me and my interviewer spent a lot of time discussing company details and general work life over there. He went about to talk about Air India’s history and all. I utilized this chance to praise the company (cause I liked it and also cause I wanted the job).

I did well overall and was pretty sure I’ll be qualifying to the next and final round.

## Managerial HR

This was the final round and almost everyone who got into this round was selected. This was done by the tech manager and it was pretty chill. Wanted to see if we had any questions about the company and all. Shared a bit about my extracurricular activities as well.

## So basically these are my tips to get through

Practice Leetcode problems in the medium section, have a good grasp of OS, DBMS, COA, DSA, Algorithm Analysis sections and frame a balanced resume in which you have a very good grasp on all topics.

But most importantly and in general for all placements, understand what type of company you want, what package you want, wait patiently and be always clear minded in making decisions. Avoid unnecessary panics and prepare with calmness and consistency.
