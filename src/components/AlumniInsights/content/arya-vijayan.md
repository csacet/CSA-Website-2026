## Round 1: Online test (4th August 2024)

The test was conducted on the SHL platform. Each section was timed. Questions cannot be skipped until they are answered, and we cannot return to previous questions. 

### Aptitude

- Three sections were there: Arithmetic, Verbal reasoning and Logical reasoning.
- Each section had around 10 questions and 10 mins.
- For arithmetic reasoning, questions were from topics like work and wages, percentage, profit and loss, time and speed, boat(upstream and downstream) etc. Similar questions can be practiced from GeeksforGeeks and Indiabix.
- Verbal reasoning and logical reasoning sections had passage questions for data interpretation and a few basic reasoning questions. 

### Technical

- This section had around 30-40 MCQ questions and 40 mins.
- All core subjects - OS, DBMS, OOPS, CN, DS and some questions related to cloud computing.

## Round 4: Interview (12th August 2024)

### Interview 1

- Interview took about 15-20 mins with two panel members.
- This round purely focused on my resume and the works I did.
- Questions related to my internships and freelance works were the primary focus.
- My portfolio contains design and development experiences, so I got questions from both domains. Questions about the tools, principles, processes, projects, and client interactions were asked.
- Technical questions were also asked in between from DBMS, CN, OS, and cybersecurity.

### Behavioural questions

- Tell me about yourself.
- Why Caterpillar?
- Why should we hire you?
- Preferred role and location etc. 

### Interview 2

- This was a technical interview round with two interviewers.
- Questions were from OOPS and I was asked to write sample codes and examples. I was using C++, so I got questions about differences between oops concepts in java and c++.
- DSA questions related to LinkedList.
- Workflow of my projects and questions related to each tech stacks used. I had one more discussion with the HR, were I got questions related to the CrowdStrike and the impact of GEN AI.
