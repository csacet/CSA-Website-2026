[Verteil](https://www.verteil.com/) Technologies is a travel technology company disrupting the 40-year-old legacy airline distribution space with its new direct connect distribution system, based on IATA’s new distribution capability (NDC). Having onboarded 30+ large Airlines and several large Travel Agents (TAs) around the Globe, Verteil is en route for rapid market capture under the new distribution model.

## ROUND 1

The first round was a written test comprising application-level questions. The assessment was open-book. One question presented a scenario involving the sorting of a 2GB file of random integers where the available RAM was limited to 1GB. We were asked to devise an algorithm to efficiently handle this situation, considering factors such as time and space complexity.

Another question required candidates to write code to determine the day corresponding to a given date. Given code we were asked to analyse time and space complexities.

Overall, the written test challenged candidates to apply their knowledge in practical scenarios, demonstrating their problem-solving abilities and proficiency in coding concepts.

## ROUND 2

The interview process comprised four rounds: three technical and one HR. In the technical interviews, questions spanned core computer science topics such as data structures, operating systems, database management systems (DBMS), computer organisation and architecture, and networking.

Additionally, candidates were quizzed on their resume projects, with questions covering areas like Docker, virtual machines, and Java programming. These rounds thoroughly evaluated candidates' understanding of fundamental concepts.
